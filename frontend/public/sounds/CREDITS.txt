Current sample library

All 19 active audio files were supplied by the project team:
- kick/: 3 WAV one-shots
- hat/: 2 WAV one-shots
- snare/: 4 WAV one-shots
- clap/: 3 WAV one-shots
- bass/: 808_hiroshima.wav, 808_low.wav, 808_mira_long.wav
- instrument/: keyboard (MP3), saxophone, flute and guitar (WAV)

Original authors, source URLs and redistribution licenses have not yet been
provided for these team-supplied files. This notice does not assign a license.

Playback changes: gain, relative pitch shifts and short fades; bass note-length
envelope and optional Cut itself. Original audio files are not modified.

Earlier prototype versions used six CC BY 3.0 recordings from
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments (Nicholaus P. Brosowsky and
contributors; original bass: Karoryfer; piano: VSO2 / Versilian Studios).
Those files are no longer used by the current catalogue. The retained
sample-source-info.txt belongs to that earlier collection, not to the current
team-supplied files.
